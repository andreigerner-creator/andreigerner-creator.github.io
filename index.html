Clip Agent

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Clip Agent is a private, personal tool used by a single creator to prepare short videos and send them as drafts to the creator's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

Terms of Service

1. Purpose

Clip Agent edits short videos and uploads them as drafts to the operator's own TikTok account using the TikTok Content Posting API. Nothing is published automatically; the account owner reviews and posts each draft manually in the TikTok app.

2. Users

The app is used only by its operator for their own TikTok account(s). No other users are accepted.

3. Acceptable use

The app is used in line with the TikTok Terms of Service, TikTok Community Guidelines and the TikTok Developer Terms. Content must respect third-party rights.

4. No warranty

The app is provided "as is", without any warranty. The operator is not liable for any damages arising from its use, to the extent permitted by law.

5. Changes

These terms may be updated at any time. The current version is always published on this page.

Privacy Policy

1. What data is processed

When the account owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives only the data allowed by the granted scopes: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), permission to upload video drafts, and, if granted, public statistics of the account's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares).

2. How it is used

This data is used only to upload drafts to the owner's account and to evaluate how the owner's own videos perform. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

3. Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the operator's own computer. No data from other TikTok users is collected.

4. Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Locally stored tokens and data are then deleted by the operator.
